Case study — grassroots club admin

From paper forms to one live system

Problem

Holiday Camp sign-ups running through paper forms and bank transfers — no visibility on who'd paid, which week, or which age group, until someone sat down and worked it out by hand.

What we built

A live registration system with secure card payments, automatic confirmation emails, and an admin dashboard showing bookings by week, age group and income in real time.

Result

Hours of manual reconciliation per camp reduced to a glance at a dashboard.
